Before you start

TalkSketch captures system audio and your microphone, sends short audio chunks to a speech-to-text service for transcription, and sends the transcript to an AI provider to write notes.

You must tell everyone present, or in the call, that an AI assistant is taking notes. Do not use this covertly.

Paste captions

Paste text or captions as Name: text, one per line. They are added to the transcript and analyzed.
