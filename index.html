The point

You talk. It keeps up.

Not another transcript dump. TalkSketch decides what is worth keeping, what deserves a sketch, and when to stay quiet.

Notes with a spine

Decisions, owners, deadlines and open questions — updated every minute, each point linked to the segment it came from.

Flags, not noise

When a name, number or deadline looks misheard, it flags the exact segment for you to verify instead of guessing.

A board that fills itself

Agenda, key points, decisions and actions land as short labels on a local Excalidraw canvas you can edit.

Action items that stick

Only explicit commitments, with the owner and deadline attached. Duplicates get merged, not repeated.

Any app, any language

Meet, Zoom, Teams, WhatsApp or a YouTube lecture — if it plays on your laptop, it listens. Language is auto-detected.

Audio isn't kept

Chunks are transcribed and discarded. Sessions stay on your own disk, and self-hosting keeps everything in your network.

Questions

Reasonable things to ask.

Is it really open source?

Yes. Apache-2.0 for the whole platform, including prompts and the whiteboard bridge. You can read every line, fork it, and run it without us.

How does it join my meeting?

It doesn't. The desktop app captures your mic and whatever audio plays on the laptop — Zoom, Meet, Teams, WhatsApp, a YouTube lecture. No bot, no invite link, nothing to install in the call.

Does it work in Bangla or other languages?

Yes. The speech model detects the language automatically, including mixed speech, and the notes are written in the language the meeting uses. Names, numbers and technical terms are kept as spoken.

What does Pro actually add?

The Windows desktop app, cloud transcription in any language, structured notes with source segments, decisions, action items, open questions, uncertainty flags, and the Excalidraw board. Self-hosting is free if you bring your own model key.

Where does my audio go?

Short audio chunks go to our transcription service, which returns text and does not store the audio. Transcript text then goes to the model provider. Self-hosting keeps everything in your network.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from your PayPal subscription page, any time. Access runs to the end of the paid period.

Why should I pay if I can self-host?

Because you would rather not run servers, manage model keys and babysit upgrades. Pro is the same software, operated for you, with the good models.
