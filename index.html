The point

You talk. It keeps up.

Not another transcript dump. TalkSketch decides what is worth keeping, what deserves a sketch, and when to stay quiet.

Notes with a spine

Decisions, numbers, owners and risks — one tight note every minute, never a wall of text.

Suggestions, not noise

It speaks up only when it can add a risk, a tradeoff or a name you forgot. Capped at three per meeting.

A board that draws itself

Agenda, key points, decisions and conclusions appear on a live Excalidraw canvas everyone can edit.

Action items that stick

Only explicit commitments, with the owner and deadline attached. Duplicates get merged, not repeated.

No bot required

Capture mic and system audio on your desktop. Or send a bot when you want one. Your call.

Private by default

Transcription runs on your machine. Cloud AI is opt-in, and self-hosting means nothing leaves your network.

Questions

Reasonable things to ask.

Is it really open source?

Yes. Apache-2.0 for the whole platform, including prompts and the whiteboard bridge. You can read every line, fork it, and run it without us.

How does it join my meeting?

Two ways: the desktop app captures your mic and system audio locally — no bot in the room. Or you can send a bot to Zoom, Meet, Teams or Jitsi, depending on your setup.

What does Pro actually add?

The Windows desktop app, local transcription, cloud notes powered by frontier models, live suggestions, and the Excalidraw board. Self-hosting is free if you bring your own model key.

Where does my audio go?

Transcription is local by default. If you use Pro, transcript text goes to our API and the model provider. Self-hosting keeps everything in your network.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from your PayPal subscription page, any time. Access runs to the end of the paid period.

Why should I pay if I can self-host?

Because you would rather not run servers, manage model keys and babysit upgrades. Pro is the same software, operated for you, with the good models.
