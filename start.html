Get started

Start in three minutes.

TalkSketch listens to whatever plays on your laptop, writes structured notes, and fills a local board — no bot joins the call and there is no meeting link to paste.

01

Install

Download the Windows installer, run it, and launch TalkSketch. SmartScreen will warn because the preview is not code-signed yet: More info → Run anyway. It installs per user, no admin required.

Download for Windows
02

Sign in

Open TalkSketch and choose Sign in with Google. Your browser opens, you approve, and the app starts a 3-day trial. No card required.

Pilot keys still work for self-hosted installs.

03

Run anything

Play the meeting in any app, press Start notes, allow screen/audio sharing and tick Share system audio. Chunks are cut at pauses, notes update about once a minute, and Stop writes the final notes.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, x64.
  • Any app with audio: Meet, Zoom, Teams, WhatsApp, a YouTube lecture.
  • A Google account for the 3-day trial.
  • Consent from participants: tell them an AI assistant is taking notes.

What is still beta

  • Audio capture uses the OS share picker: pick the window and enable system audio.
  • Transcription is cloud-based (language auto-detected); accuracy varies with accents and crosstalk.
  • Unclear names, numbers or gaps are flagged for you to verify instead of guessed.
  • No bot joins the call. Audio is transcribed, not stored; sessions stay on your disk.
