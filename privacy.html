Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

TalkSketch turns meetings into notes. This policy explains what we process, why, where it goes, and how to make us delete it. It covers the website, the desktop app, and the hosted demo.

What we collect

  • Meeting content. Transcript text, speaker labels, notes, action items and whiteboard elements. Your audio is not uploaded by the desktop preview; it reads the meeting's captions and sends text.
  • Account data. Email address, subscription status, and the access code or token used to sign in.
  • Payment data. Handled by PayPal. We receive a subscription identifier and status, never your card number.
  • Technical data. IP address, request time, and error logs, used for rate limiting and debugging.

Why we process it

  • To produce notes, decisions, action items and the whiteboard you asked for.
  • To operate subscriptions and enforce access.
  • To keep the service reliable and secure.

Who else sees it

Transcript text is sent to a model provider to generate the output. Payments go through PayPal. The site is served through Cloudflare and GitHub Pages. Our hosting provider stores the data described above. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use meeting content to train models.

Retention

Demo transcripts, notes and board images are deleted after 24 hours. Live meeting records are kept for the duration of the session and deleted on the same 24-hour schedule unless you ask us to keep them. Account and billing records are kept while your subscription is active and for as long as tax law requires. Backups rotate within 30 days.

Your choices

You can request a copy or deletion of your data, or ask us to stop processing it, by emailing privacy@talksketch.work. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. Our lawful bases are contract (delivering the service), legitimate interests (security and reliability), and consent (where a participant is told an AI assistant is present).

Recording and consent

You are responsible for telling participants that an AI assistant is transcribing the meeting, and for obtaining any consent required where you live. Do not use TalkSketch to record people covertly.

Cookies and local storage

The site does not use advertising cookies. The desktop app stores your server address, access code and preferences in local storage on your machine.

Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. Material changes will be announced by email to subscribers.
